❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How accurate is the Placement AI Predictor? +

The predictor uses a statistical model based on CGPA, college tier, internship experience, backlogs, and skill count. It provides a range estimate with ~85–90% directional accuracy. For exact figures, consult your college placement cell.

How do I use the Score Oracle? +

Navigate to the Score Oracle tab, select your target exam (GATE/CAT/CUET), enter your average mock score, months of preparation, tap on your strong subjects, and hit Predict. You'll get a score range and institute eligibility.

Can I compare more than 4 colleges? +

Currently the Compare tool supports up to 4 colleges side-by-side. This covers the most common decision-making scenarios. Multi-set comparison is on the roadmap.

Is my data stored anywhere? +

PlaceIQ runs entirely in your browser session. No data is sent to any server. Your profile exists only until you close the tab — purely client-side for now.

How do I retake the Quick Tour? +

Click the Take Tour button in the top navigation bar anytime to restart the guided walkthrough of PlaceIQ's features.
